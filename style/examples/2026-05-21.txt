### 🌋 Shaky Grounds in Peru

"Moving over to South America, the Earth gave us a pretty big reminder that it is alive and moving!"

* **The Rumble:** A powerful **5.8 magnitude** earthquake rattled the Pacific region of Peru.
> **What is Magnitude?** It's the scale scientists use to measure how much energy an earthquake releases. A 5.8 means the ground shook hard enough to wobble buildings and cause some damage!


* **The Good News:** While 27 people were unfortunately injured, absolutely no deaths were reported. Peru sits right on the **Andes Fault** (a giant crack in the Earth's crust where tectonic plates grind past each other), making it a prime spot for regular shake-ups!

### 🍵 Raising a Cup for International Tea Day!

"And finally, for some delicious news—put your pinkies up, because May 21st is officially **International Tea Day**!

* **Supporting Farmers:** This day helps us learn how to support the hardworking farmers who grow and harvest tea leaves so we can enjoy everything from iced green tea to warm chamomile!

## 🚀 Category 4: Space News

"Look up, space cadets! Our final stop today is the stars. NASA and SpaceX are making massive moves to explore deep space and build cities on the Moon!"

### 🌌 The Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope

"NASA is officially fast-tracking its next giant eye in the sky, aiming for a launch as early as September 2026!"

* **Data Avalanche:** Once it's in space, this telescope is going to collect a mind-boggling **20,000 terabytes of data** over its 5-year mission. That is equal to packing a million high-definition movies into one satellite!
* **Cosmic Mysteries:** The Roman Telescope will search for hidden exoplanets, map distant galaxies, and help scientists unlock the secrets of dark energy.

### 🌞 Unlocking the Sun's Coronal Holes

"Scientists have just used a brilliant new AI tool to map the mysterious dark patches on the Sun called **coronal holes**."

* **Cosmic Fireworks:** Coronal holes are areas where the Sun’s magnetic field opens up, allowing fast-moving particles called **solar wind** to shoot out into space like celestial fireworks.
* **3D Mapping:** Using images from the Solar Dynamics Observatory satellite, the new AI maps these holes in 3D. This helps scientists predict space weather before it hits Earth, protecting our satellites and giving us beautiful views of the Northern Lights!

### 🌕 40 Priorities for a Moon City

"NASA has officially locked in 40 technology priorities to build permanent infrastructure at the Moon's South Pole. They are literally designing a space station on the lunar surface!"

> **What is a Space Station?** Think of a space station as a high-tech, sealed house and laboratory floating in space or sitting on another world. It provides air to breathe, power to run equipment, and a safe place for astronauts to live, eat, sleep, and do science experiments where humans normally couldn't survive!

"To build this ultimate lunar base, NASA is focusing on points like:"

* **Power Up! ⚡** Engineering special energy systems to keep the lights and heaters running through the freezing, two-week-long lunar nights.
* **Robot Builders 🤖** Creating smart, autonomous robots that can dig, move heavy boulders, and assemble buildings without human steering.
* **Using Moon Dirt 🏗️** Developing ways to mix lunar regolith (Moon dust) to create concrete so we can build structures right out of the ground instead of flying heavy bricks all the way from Earth.
* **Dust Busters 🌌** Inventing static-shields to stop sharp, pesky Moon dust from clogging up spacesuits and machinery.
* **Gateway to Mars 🔴** Everything we learn from building this Moon base will be used as a practice run to eventually send the very first humans to Mars!
