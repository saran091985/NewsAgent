3.Wait, What’s a "Carbon Credit"?
The Deal: Singapore and the Philippines just signed a huge deal to trade "Carbon Credits."  This deal allows them to share and trade these Carbon Credits!

So, what are carbon credits?
 Well, think of them like superhero badges for companies! When businesses produce carbon dioxide (a gas that can be harmful to our planet), they can buy carbon credits to show they’re helping to reduce pollution. These credits mean they’re supporting projects that help clean the air, like planting trees or using clean energy.

This deal is part of something called the Paris Agreement, which is a global effort where countries come together to tackle climate change.
