🌍 WORLD NEWS
1. The Iran-US Conflict: A Big Disagreement

What’s Happening: Things are getting very "heated" between Israel, Lebanon, and Iran. Israel has asked families in Southern Lebanon to leave their homes quickly to stay safe because of a group called Hezbollah.

The Peace Problem: Former U.S. President Donald Trump said he isn't happy with a new peace plan from Iran. It’s like two kids trying to make up after a fight, but one says, "No, that's not a fair deal yet."

Staying Safe: Everyone is hoping the leaders can stop fighting and start talking so people can go back to their comfy beds and favorite toys.

2. Japan’s Brave Firefighters vs. The Mega-Fire

The Battle: In northern Japan (a place called Iwate), one of the biggest wildfires in decades broke out! It’s been burning for 11 whole days.

Superheroes in Action: Over 1,000 firefighters are working like real-life superheroes to stop the flames.

The Size: The fire burned over 1,176 hectares—that is like 1,600 soccer fields! Let's give a big cheer for the brave teams keeping the towns safe. 🚒

3. The Oil Team Meeting (OPEC+)

The Team: OPEC+ is a group of countries that decide how much oil to make. We need oil for cars and to make electricity!

The News: They decided to make a little bit more oil (188,000 barrels more a day), but one big player—the UAE—didn't join the vote this time.

4. The Great Car Fee (Tariffs)

The Rule: Donald Trump announced a 25% "tariff" on cars coming from Europe.

Wait, what’s a Tariff? It’s an extra tax or fee. If a car costs $100, you have to pay an extra $25 just to bring it into the country. It’s meant to encourage people to buy cars made in the USA!

5. China Stands Its Ground

The Conflict: The U.S. told China they shouldn't trade with certain countries (these rules are called Sanctions).The U.S. set up some of these rules against China, telling them not to buy certain things from places like Russia and Iran.

The Response: China said "No thank you!" and decided to keep doing business anyway. It’s a giant game of international tug-of-war!

What are Sanctions? 🚫
Sanctions are like "economic time-outs." One country stops trading or sharing money with another country to try and make them change their behavior.

🇮🇳 INDIA NEWS
6. Air India’s Summer Schedule Change

The News: Air India is cutting down on some big international trips to places like the USA and Europe.

The Reason: Airplane fuel is getting very expensive. To save money, they are flying fewer times—like changing a flight from 10 times a week down to 7.

Don't Worry: You can still travel, you just have to plan a little more carefully! ✈️

💻 TECHNOLOGY NEWS
7. Meet RAVEN: The AI Planet Hunter

The Tool: Scientists at the University of Warwick made a super-smart AI (Artificial Intelligence) named RAVEN.

The Mission: RAVEN acts like a robot detective, looking through data from the TESS space telescope to find hidden planets.

The Score: It just confirmed over 100 exoplanets! 31 of them are brand new discoveries. AI is helping us see things that humans might miss!

What is an Exoplanet? 🪐
An exoplanet is any planet that is outside our solar system. It doesn't circle our Sun; it circles a different star far, far away!

🚀 SPACE NEWS
8. SpaceX’s Giant Satellite Carpool

The Rocket: A SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket launched from California.

The Cargo: It carried 45 satellites at once! This is called a Rideshare Mission.

Why it matters: Instead of one rocket for one satellite, they "carpool" to save money. These satellites help us with GPS (so we don't get lost) and the internet!

9. The Moon Travelers are Back!

The Stars: The four astronauts of the Artemis II mission are touring the world to tell their stories.

The Mission: They flew around the Moon! This is a practice run before NASA sends people to actually walk on the Moon again.

Fun Fact: They spent 10 days floating in zero gravity. Imagine trying to eat a taco while it's floating away from you! 🌮🛰️
