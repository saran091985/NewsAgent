## ⚙️ Technology News: Super Speed and Space Computers

9.The "Superhero" Missile:**
* The **DRDO** (India's science team) tested a **Hypersonic Missile** using a "Scramjet" engine! 🚀
* **Technical Detail:** This missile travels at **Hypersonic speed**, which means it goes way faster than the speed of sound (about 5 times faster!).
* **What’s a Scramjet?** It’s a special engine that "breathes" air from the sky to help it zoom without carrying heavy oxygen tanks! 🏃‍♂️💨

10.A Computer in the Stars:**
* India launched the **Pathfinder**, its first "orbital data centre" satellite.
* **The Magic Part:** Usually, satellites just take pictures and send them down. Pathfinder can **process and analyze** the data while it’s still in space! It's like having a super-brain flying over our heads! 🌠💫
